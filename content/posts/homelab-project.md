## Homelab Project - But why?
I created a homelab to learn new things about DevOps. That is obvious.

But why?
Because at work I can't try everything. Even our development staging has dependencies, and there are other developers involved. :)

I want to explore new technologies in peace. I want to make mistakes.
So I bought a second-hand laptop and enrolled in an Udemy course for CKA (Certified Kubernetes Administrator).

Most importantly, I will share my findings and personal experience.

So it begins!
